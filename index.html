    How it plugs into Google Workspace

    Assumes ATG runs Google Workspace. I would confirm tooling in week one and adapt. See the automation roadmap

    Google Sheets: source of truth

    • One row per obligation: region, entity, owner, due date, status, evidence link.
    • Dropdown statuses, conditional formatting for overdue, protected columns.

    Google Calendar: reminders

    • One shared calendar per region, generated from the Sheet.
    • Tiered alerts at 60, 30, and 7 days; the owner is added as a guest.

    Google Chat: nudges

    • A compliance-alerts space plus one space per region.
    • A weekly digest via Apps Script webhook: due in 14 days, overdue, blocked, with @owner mentions.

    Google Drive: evidence

    • Folder per year and obligation (filings, I-9 audits, training certificates).
    • Role-based sharing and retention labels; doubles as SOC 2 evidence.

    Google Forms: attestations

    • Handbook and policy acknowledgments, manager sign-offs, leave notices.
    • Responses land in a Sheet that feeds the dashboard.

    Looker Studio and Groups

    • Looker Studio reads the Sheet and HRIS exports for the dashboard.
    • Google Groups drive access, so joiner and leaver changes flow to the right systems.

    Guardrails

    • Automation reminds, routes, and records. People decide and sign off on filings, I-9 corrections, and anything with legal exposure.
    • Sensitive documents (I-9 files, SSNs, medical details) stay in approved systems. AI tools only see redacted or non-sensitive text unless ATG's security team approves otherwise.
    • Every automation has a named owner, a log, and a way to switch it off.

                              Where this connects to ATG's growth drivers

                              ATG's own six drivers, and the two this plan supports most directly.

                              Pursue accretive M&A

                              A repeatable integration playbook (payroll, benefits, policies, right to work, access) means the next acquisition adds capability instead of adding to the acquisition debt.

                              Expand operational leverage

                              Fewer payrolls, one core handbook, one calendar, and one dashboard let People Ops scale across regions without adding a team for every acquisition.

                              The other four drivers

                              Growing the addressable market, conversion, the network effect, and take rate all depend on teams in the US, UK, EU, and Mexico being hired, onboarded, and retained consistently.

                              Ground rules so events include everyone

                              • No required event outside local working hours, and every live event is recorded with a written recap.
                              • Captions on, and Spanish and other local-language support where it is needed.
                              • A shared holiday calendar for the US, UK, EU countries, and Mexico, so nothing lands on a local holiday.
                              • Budgets and perks set by country with Finance and tax advice, since gifts and stipends can be taxable.
                              • Voluntary and varied: not every event is a happy hour, and none depends on alcohol.
                              • Start with a listening round (survey plus small group chats) before launching anything.
